HOW IT IS WALKED
Each act is walked in the voyage's own world — the same earth, the same blocks, the same sky and folk — at the place it happened: Natsareth in the hills of the Galil, Kephar Naḥum of black basalt on the shore of the lake, the Yardĕn below Yeriḥo, and the city of the great king as she stood in those days.

You walk among the events as a witness. Named figures say only what the Besorah says — every word is the Besorah’s, with its reference beneath it; your own lines are your reactions, and are marked as yours. Notes on dates and empires are history, and marked as notes.
